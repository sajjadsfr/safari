§ 01, About

A short
biography.

Fig. 01, Newark, DE 2025

I'm a computer vision and structural AI researcher completing my Ph.D. at the University of Delaware, advised by Prof. Monique Head in the HEAD Research Group. My dissertation, “Formulations for Data-Fusion-Enhanced Deployment of Digital Image Correlation Systems in Structural Evaluation,” builds end-to-end measurement pipelines that extract structural state from ordinary camera footage: dense sub-pixel motion tracking, multi-modal sensor fusion, signal processing, and physics-informed finite element model calibration, deployed in the field on bridges, culverts, railways, and coastal structures.

I approach problems from both ends: I design and execute field investigations (five structures across four states, under live traffic, active rail load, and tidal cycles), and I build the computational frameworks that turn raw vision data into defensible engineering assessments. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, applied signal processing, and structural mechanics.

Born in Tehran in 1993, I trained as a civil engineer at the University of Tehran before moving to Newark, DE in 2021. I run, a marathon under the belt, take photographs (a habit since 2014), and pay attention to music and art as the rest of the rhythm.

I am seeking roles in applied computer vision, AI for physical systems, and technical consulting, particularly where rigorous measurement, field judgment, and computational thinking need to work together.

§ 02, Research

Dissertation
Working title · defending Aug 2026

Formulations for Data-Fusion-Enhanced Deployment of Digital Image Correlation Systems in Structural Evaluation.

Short form, a data-fusion-driven approach for structural evaluation using DIC.

I develop computer vision systems for quantitative structural assessment, using Digital Image Correlation (DIC) to extract displacement, strain, and dynamic response from image sequences, then fusing that data with accelerometers and physics-based finite element models to characterize structural state and calibrate load ratings. The work covers the full pipeline: field instrumentation and deployment, signal processing and virtual sensor synthesis, data fusion architectures, and FE model updating. Applied to bridges, culverts, rail, and coastal infrastructure under real-world load conditions.

Advised by Prof. Monique Head in the Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering at the University of Delaware.
